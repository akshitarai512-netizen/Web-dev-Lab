Students Win National Coding Hackathon

By Staff Reporter

A team of first-year students from the college has won first place at a national hackathon, beating more than two hundred teams. Their project, a study planner built with web technologies, impressed the judges with its design and usefulness.

The team spent thirty-six hours building the project and plans to improve it further with feedback from their classmates.
